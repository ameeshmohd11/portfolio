# Neonatal Smart Incubator

An IoT-driven **Smart Neonatal Incubator Monitoring System** designed to continuously track critical physiological vitals and environmental metrics for premature and fragile newborns.

---

## Project Overview

Premature infants require continuous, precise temperature regulation, humidity control, and non-stop monitoring. The Smart Incubator integrates embedded IoT sensors with an intelligent dashboard to alert medical staff instantly upon detecting abnormal parameters.

---

## Core Capabilities

- **Environmental Sensing**: Real-time ambient temperature, humidity, air quality, and oxygen saturation monitoring.
- **Vital Signs Tracking**: Continuous infant heart rate, body temperature, and movement tracking.
- **Automated Alarm & Alert System**: Triggers immediate visual and auditory alerts when vitals exceed safe thresholds.
- **Telemetry Dashboard**: Live telemetry streaming for nurses and doctors.

---

## Hardware & Tech Stack

- **Microcontroller / Sensors**: Arduino / ESP32 / DHT22 / Pulse Sensor / Temp Probes
- **Connectivity**: IoT protocols, MQTT / Wi-Fi telemetry
