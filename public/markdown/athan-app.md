# Athan Desktop App

A lightweight and elegant desktop application built with **WPF & C# (.NET)** for calculating Islamic prayer times, displaying live countdowns to upcoming prayers, and triggering timely Athan audio alerts.

---

## Features

- **Accurate Prayer Calculations**: Computes Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha timings based on location and standard calculation methods.
- **Live Countdown Timer**: Real-time ticker showing the exact time remaining until the next prayer.
- **Audio Notifications**: Configurable Athan audio callouts when prayer times arrive.
- **Customizable Settings**: Adjust calculation methods, Juristic conventions (Shafi/Hanafi), and coordinate settings.
- **Clean UI**: Modern and unobtrusive desktop widget design with dark and light styling.
